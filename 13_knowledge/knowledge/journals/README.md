# knowledge/journals/ — Journals

## What Is a Journal?

A **journal** is a daily record of what happened, what was learned, and what was decided.

Journals capture context that would otherwise be lost:
- Why did you take that trade?
- What were you thinking when you made that decision?
- What unexpected things happened?

---

## Why Journals Exist

Six months from now, you will not remember what you were thinking today. Journals are your past self talking to your future self.

They are especially valuable for:
- Reviewing past decisions
- Identifying patterns in your thinking
- Providing context to AI agents
- Measuring personal and system improvement over time

---

## Format

```
journals/YYYY-MM-DD.md
```
