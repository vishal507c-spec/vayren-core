# knowledge/references/ — References

## What Are References?

**References** are external resources that inform the company's work: research papers, articles, books, documentation, and tools.

---

## Why This Exists

When you find a valuable resource:
- You might need it again
- You might want to share it with future collaborators
- The AI might need to reference it

By keeping an annotated list, references become searchable and accessible.
